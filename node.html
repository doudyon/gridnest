Meet the GridNest AI Node — a managed computing unit designed to bring AI infrastructure into everyday spaces.

THE IDEA

A computer that works
for a whole network.

A GridNest Node is a powerful GPU computer installed at an eligible host location and managed as part of a distributed computing network. Instead of every company buying its own AI hardware, computing capacity can be made available to organizations that need it.

INSIDE THE NODE

Meet the hardware.

WHY COMPANIES PAY FOR GPU COMPUTE

AI needs more than software.
It needs processing power.

AI models perform vast numbers of calculations. GPUs handle many of these operations in parallel, making them useful for running models, processing images and video, simulations and rendering. Companies pay for access to this computing capacity because owning and maintaining powerful hardware is expensive, especially when demand changes.

A DIFFERENT INFRASTRUCTURE MODEL

Why rent a whole data center's overhead?

Large data centers require substantial investment in buildings, power delivery, cooling, redundancy, security and ongoing operations. Those costs are reflected in the price of computing services.

GridNest explores a distributed alternative: placing managed compute units in suitable existing spaces. By using available locations rather than building a large centralized facility, the model aims to reduce certain infrastructure costs and offer competitive GPU capacity for workloads that fit distributed deployment.

Actual cost savings depend on electricity, cooling, connectivity, hardware utilization, maintenance and service requirements. Some workloads still need centralized data centers.
