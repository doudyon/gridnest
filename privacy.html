This policy describes how GridNest handles information submitted through this website. Last updated: October 21, 2026.

01 / Information we collect

When you submit a hosting application or business inquiry, we collect the details you provide, such as your name, contact information, location or company, property and connectivity information, and the content of your message.

02 / How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, assess hosting eligibility, discuss potential partnerships, manage requests and maintain the security and operation of our website. We do not use form submissions to enroll you in marketing messages without an appropriate basis.

03 / Storage and service providers

Form submissions are processed through our hosting infrastructure and stored using Google Firebase / Firestore. Email notifications about inquiries may be sent using Resend. These providers may process data on our behalf in accordance with their applicable terms and safeguards, potentially outside your country.

04 / Sharing and retention

We share information with service providers as needed to operate the website and handle inquiries, or when required by law. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for these purposes, legal obligations and legitimate recordkeeping, then delete or anonymize it as appropriate.

05 / Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information. To make a privacy request, use our business contact form and identify it as a privacy inquiry.

06 / Security and updates

We take reasonable measures to protect submitted information, although no online service can guarantee absolute security. We may update this policy from time to time; the latest version will be posted here.
